Vehicle Planning Tools Demo

Two working tools: a rolling 12-month demand plan with proposed orders, and a check of scanned customs cards against the vehicle master list. All figures in the demos are dummy data. Files are processed in your browser and are not uploaded anywhere.
Tool 1

Demand Planner

Recalculates closing stock and stock cover for every trim and proposes the orders needed to reach the target cover.

  • Input from the planning Excel file
  • Cover heatmap and order proposals by trim
  • Planner overrides and Excel export
Open Demand Planner → Download Excel model
Tool 2

VIN Arrival Checker

Reads the VIN on each scanned customs card and compares it with the master list.

  • Arrived, not arrived and not in master
  • Page number, card date and card number per vehicle
  • Doubtful cards shown with the card image for review
Open VIN Arrival Checker →
