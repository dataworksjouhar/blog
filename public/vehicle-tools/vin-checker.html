How the check works
  1. Each page is read in three ways, in order: hidden text inside the PDF, the QR code, then text recognition (OCR) on the image. The first method that gives a reliable VIN is used.
  2. For OCR, the page is turned to each orientation until a VIN is found. Only Latin characters are needed, so Arabic text is ignored.
  3. A VIN is 17 characters and never contains I, O or Q. Misreads such as O for 0 are corrected automatically.
  4. The 9th character is a check digit. A VIN that fails it was misread, and the tool tries the most common one-character corrections.
  5. A read that passes the check digit is never changed to fit the master list, because two real vehicles can differ by one character.
  6. A VIN within 2 characters of one master VIN is shown as a probable match for a person to confirm, next to the image of the card.
